Demonstration configuration: 14 calendar days per stage, three-day advance reminders, one declared-available EVC member as demo quorum, Asia/Kolkata date boundaries. None is claimed as an AICTE requirement. Prior version uses ten days to demonstrate case pinning. SAC and SHC remain distinct configurable stages. The platform supports authorized human verification and decisions; it does not constitute statutory committees.

Before any official use: policy owner must verify applicability by category/cycle, clauses/pages, effective periods, revised calendars, appointments, required evidence and every deadline. No live authorized government adapter exists.
